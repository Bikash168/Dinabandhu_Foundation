> **Est. 1999 • Bhubaneswar, Odisha**  
> *Nurturing Healthy Communities, Resilient Ecosystems & Skilled Lives*  
> Registered Trust under the **Indian Trust Act 1882** • **Section 12AA & 80G Certified** under the Indian Income Tax Act 1961.

---

## 🌟 Overview

This is a modern, responsive web application built with **Next.js 14 (App Router)**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS** for **Dinabandhu Foundation for Educational Research and Socio Economic Development**.

The website faithfully incorporates the foundation's official branding, emblem/logo, and comprehensive content spanning its 27+ year legacy, founder heritage, governance, 3 core developmental pillars (24 grassroots focus areas), Odisha operational footprint across 10 districts, and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) apex integration.

---

## 🏛️ Key Foundation Verticals & Pillars

### Integrated Impact Verticals:
1. **Trident Academy of Technology**: Flagship higher & technical engineering, computer applications, and biotechnology college under the trust (10,000+ graduates).
2. **SkillEM**: The vocational training arm implementing flagship Central & State government schemes such as **DDU-GKY** and **PMKVY**.
3. **Indevin**: The grassroots welfare brand and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) apex body delivering transparent, audited social interventions.

### Three Developmental Pillars (24 Focus Areas):
- **01 • Public Health & Nutrition**: Community health literacy, mobile diagnostic check-ups, maternal & infant nutrition, safe water & sanitation, and rural health outreach.
- **02 • Environment & Climate Action**: Native tree plantation, coastal climate risk resilience, rainwater harvesting, decentralized waste composting, and school eco-clubs.
- **03 • Skill Development & Livelihoods**: Demand-driven trades, green economy skills (solar, waste, water audits), digital literacy, women SHG micro-enterprise coaching, and employment linkages.

### Geographic Footprint in Odisha:
- **10 Districts**: Balasore, Bhadrak, Kendrapara, Jagatsinghpur, Puri, Khordha, Jajpur, Ganjam, Koraput, Nuapada.
- **Reach**: 20 dedicated field members, 200+ villages, and 10,000+ community members impacted.
- **Vision**: UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs 1, 3, 10, 13), **Developed Odisha by 2036**, and **Viksit Bharat by 2047**.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites:
- Node.js 18+ (tested with Node.js v26.13)
- npm or pnpm

### Running Development Server:
```bash
npm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

### Building for Production:
```bash
npm run build
npm start
```
